Payment Policy

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
We do not accept Discover, Google Pay, or gift cards as payment methods.

Only one discount code can be used per order.
Discount codes cannot be combined with other promotions.

Payment is processed at the time of order placement.
For subscription orders, payment is processed automatically on the renewal date.

Customers can update their payment method in their account settings.
Payment methods cannot be changed after an order is placed.
