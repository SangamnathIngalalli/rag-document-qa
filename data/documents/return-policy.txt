Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
To initiate a return, customers must provide their order number.
Items must be in original condition with all packaging.

Defective items are eligible for return within 30 days of purchase.
Defective returns are free of charge.
Non-defective returns may incur a small shipping fee.

Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
Damaged items will be replaced at no cost to the customer.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
