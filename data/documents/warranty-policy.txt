Warranty Policy

Most electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty.
Some premium items come with extended warranties of 2 or 3 years.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage.
Warranty claims must be filed with the manufacturer, not the retailer.

Customers should keep their proof of purchase for warranty claims.
Warranty does not cover normal wear and tear.

For warranty questions, customers should contact the manufacturer directly.
